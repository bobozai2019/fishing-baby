# ImageGen 提示词集合

生成模式：内置 ImageGen；原玩法图作为设计与构图参考，不作为需要保留的编辑底图。

## 鱼类双状态模板

> Production-ready flat-color 2D game sprite pose pair. Create exactly two isolated full-body poses of the same fish, horizontally arranged: left is a normal right-facing swimming pose; right is the same fish caught and struggling, body arched, distressed expression, mouth open and tail strongly bent. Preserve identical species, proportions, colors, markings, outline thickness and scale. American cartoon casual-game sprite with bold dark outlines and flat solid fills. No hook, rope or scenery. No highlights, shine, rim light, glow, shadow, ambient lighting, gradients, painterly shading or 3D rendering. Uniform chroma-key background.

鱼类主题：小型蓝鱼、绿色杂鱼、黄色条纹鱼、紫色巨型鱼、橙红稀有鱼。

## 独立元素模板

> Production-ready isolated 2D game sprite matching the reference concept. One complete centered subject with generous padding, bold clean dark outlines, exaggerated American-cartoon silhouette and a small flat palette. No highlight, specular patch, rim light, glow, bloom, cast/contact shadow, ambient light, gradient, painterly shading or 3D rendering. Perfectly uniform #00FF00 chroma-key background; no floor, water, bubbles, text or extra objects.

独立元素主题：船+渔夫+吊机、三爪抓钩、螃蟹、水母、开启的宝箱、珍珠贝、旧靴子、轮胎、压扁的罐头、海底装饰组合、海面波浪条。

## 背景模板

> Production-ready 16:9 flat-color side-view ocean cross-section. Top 28% sky, lower 72% underwater, small distant lighthouse island at upper-right, simple sandy seabed and low distant decoration silhouettes at the bottom edges. Broad center left empty for gameplay. No fish, pickups, boat, hook, HUD or text. No sun, rays, caustics, highlights, glow, shadows, lighting gradients, depth gradients or painterly shading. Use only uniform flat graphic regions.
